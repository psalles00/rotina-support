privacy

Your data

Routines, steps and progress are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, this data syncs privately across your devices on every plan. Appearance preferences stay on this device.

Calendar and alarms

Calendar access is only requested when you enable integration. Rotina only reads the selected calendars to display events and uses AlarmKit and local notifications for the reminders you set up.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by App Store. Rotina only checks the status of the purchase necessary to release the Premium resources.

Retention and deletion

You can delete routines and their records in the app. Local data remains until deleted or the app is removed. Previously synced data may remain in iCloud; manage Rotina storage in your Apple Account settings.

Rotina keeps a local copy of displayed events while calendar integration is enabled. Disabling integration or revoking permission clears this cache without deleting Calendar events. You can revoke permissions in device Settings.

Support

When sending a support email, you choose which information to share. Your email app sends the message to pedrosalles00@gmail.com. You can also send privacy requests to this address.
